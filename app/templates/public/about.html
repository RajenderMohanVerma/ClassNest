What {{ app_name }} is

{{ app_name }} is a class-based learning library by {{ teacher_name }}. Its catalog follows a clear structure: Class → Subject → Chapter → Content. Browse published material and check each page for its access details.

  • A browsable class catalogMove from class to subject, chapter, and individual resource.
  • Different study formatsPublished material may include notes, PDFs, video, audio, links, and images.
  • Access checked on the serverPublished status and account permissions are checked before protected material is served.

Getting started

  1. Choose a class and open one of its published subjects.
  2. Browse chapters or search by a topic, title, or keyword.
  3. Open a resource and review its availability and access requirements.
  4. Create a student account if a resource requires sign-in.
