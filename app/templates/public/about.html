What {{ app_name }} is

{{ app_name }} is a study library built around one simple structure: Class → Subject → Chapter → Content. Every note, video, PDF and audio file sits in exactly one place, so you can always find it again by the same route.

  • Free notes, PDFs and video lessons
  • Full courses with sections and lessons
  • Progress, bookmarks and notifications for students
  • Installable as an app and usable offline for cached pages

Getting started

  1. Pick your class and open the subject you are studying.
  2. Work through the chapters in order, or jump to a topic with search.
  3. Create a free account to save your progress and bookmark resources.
  4. Upgrade to a premium course when you want the complete series for a subject.
