What we collect

When you create an account we store your name, email address and a hashed password. We never store your password in readable form and we never store card numbers. Payment confirmation is recorded as an order reference only.

Your learning activity — course enrolments, lesson progress, bookmarks and notification read state — is stored so the platform can show your own dashboard. That data is visible only to you and to the teacher account.

Cookies

A single session cookie keeps you signed in. It is marked HttpOnly and SameSite so scripts cannot read it, and it is sent over HTTPS only in production. There is no advertising or cross-site tracking cookie.

Uploaded files and premium media

Files are stored outside the public static folder. They are not reachable by guessing a URL: every download is checked against your account status, class permission and, for premium material, a verified purchase.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not send marketing email unless you asked for it.
  • We do not display fabricated reviews, ratings or student counts.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your account and its learning records at any time through the contact page. Orders are retained for accounting purposes even after an account is closed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact form.
