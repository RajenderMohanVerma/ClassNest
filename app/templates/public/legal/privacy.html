What we collect

When you create an account we store your name, email address, a password hash, and any optional profile details you provide. We do not store your password in readable form. Payment details are handled by the configured payment provider; ClassNext records transaction references needed to track an order.

We also store information you submit through the site, such as contact enquiries and your selected class. Account and enquiry records are used to operate the requested site features and are available to authorized teacher accounts for support and administration.

Cookies

A single session cookie keeps you signed in. It is marked HttpOnly and SameSite so scripts cannot read it, and it is sent over HTTPS only in production. There is no advertising or cross-site tracking cookie.

Uploaded files and premium media

Files are stored outside the public static folder. They are not reachable by guessing a URL: every download is checked against your account status, class permission and, for premium material, a verified purchase.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not send marketing email unless you asked for it.
  • We do not display fabricated reviews, ratings or student counts.

Your choices

For a question about the information associated with your account, use the contact page. Requests are reviewed by the teacher. Transaction records, if any, may need to be retained where required for accounting or legal purposes.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact form.
