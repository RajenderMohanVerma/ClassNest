Scope

This policy applies to premium course purchases made on {{ app_name }}. Free material needs no refund because nothing was charged.

Before you buy

Each course page shows the price, the number of lessons and which lessons are free previews. You can read the previews and the course outline before paying, and you can view any free lessons on the site without buying anything.

Refunds are granted

  • You were charged and the course was never granted because of a fault on our side.
  • You bought the same course twice by mistake.
  • The course was withdrawn and no equivalent material is offered.
  • You purchased in error within 7 days and have not completed any lesson.

Refunds are not granted

  • You have completed a significant portion of the lessons.
  • The request is made more than 14 days after purchase.
  • The reason is a change of mind after starting the course.
  • The content is still available and working as described.

How to request one

  1. Use the contact page with your order number.
  2. We confirm the order on the server against the recorded payment reference.
  3. If approved, access is revoked and the refund is issued to the original payment method.

Duplicate charges

A repeated payment notification never creates a second purchase. If you see a duplicated charge on your statement, contact us with both references and we will resolve it.
